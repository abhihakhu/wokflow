# ADR 0004 — Visual direction

- **Status:** accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-24

## Decision

An original traditional-Chinese harbor kitchen at dusk: grey tiled eaves, red-lacquer beams and pillars with gold trim, wooden lattice walls, a moon-gate window onto ink-wash mountains, paper lanterns, chili and garlic strings, blue-and-white porcelain, a brick stove with a live fire and a wok.

UI materials: parchment scroll panels with a red key-pattern border, red-lacquer buttons with a gold inner line, dark-wood plaques, a jade-ring timer.

Type: Noto Serif SC (headings, Latin + CJK), Nunito Sans (body), Ma Shan Zheng (seal accents such as the 锅 logo).
